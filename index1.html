GeoSection AI Guide — By Hawber Ata

1. Per-Layer Dips & Unconformities: Expand any layer in the sidebar to adjust its individual dip ($-60^\circ$ to $+60^\circ$) or mark it as an angular unconformity truncating underlying strata.

2. Synclines & Flexible Folding: Choose between Synclines (troughs/basins), Anticlines (crests/arches), Asymmetric folds, and Monoclines with adjustable hinge positions and ductile deformation flow.

3. Saving on Android: Tap Export as PNG to open the rendered graphic; long-press the image to save directly to your phone gallery.
