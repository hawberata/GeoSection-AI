GeoSection AI Guide — By Hawber Ata

1. Dip-Aligned USGS Lithology Patterns: All USGS patterns (Limestone bricks, Dolomite blocks, Shale dashes, Sandstone bedding) automatically rotate to align with each unit's exact dip angle.

2. Fast Field Station Entry: In Field Traverse mode, the + Add Next Station Leg button is located right below the station list so you can add 10+ stations sequentially without scrolling back up.

3. Saving on Android: Tap Export as PNG to open the rendered graphic; long-press the image to save directly to your phone gallery.
